# Nexus Technologies Ltd. - Global Human Resources Policy Manual

**Document Control Information**
- **Policy Number:** HR-POL-2026-001
- **Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
- **Last Revised:** May 25, 2026
- **Policy Owner:** Global Head of Human Resources
- **Applies To:** All full-time, part-time, temporary employees, and independent contractors (collectively referred to as "Employees").

---

## 1.0 Purpose and Scope
The purpose of this Human Resources Policy Manual is to establish the overarching principles, rules, and procedures governing employment at Nexus Technologies Ltd. ("the Company"). This policy ensures compliance with local and international labor laws, promotes a safe and inclusive work environment, and provides a framework for organizational consistency. 

This policy supersedes all previous oral and written policies and guidelines regarding the subjects covered herein.

---

## 2.0 Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) and Anti-Discrimination
Nexus Technologies Ltd. is strictly an equal opportunity employer. We base all employment decisions on merit, qualifications, and business needs.
1. **Protected Characteristics:** The Company strictly prohibits discrimination or harassment based on race, color, religion, age, sex, national origin, disability status, genetics, protected veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, or any other characteristic protected by federal, state, or local laws.
2. **Reasonable Accommodations:** The Company will make reasonable accommodations for qualified individuals with known disabilities unless doing so would result in an undue hardship on the Company's operations.

---

## 3.0 Working Hours and Remote Work (Hybrid Model)
1. **Core Hours:** The standard workweek consists of 40 hours. Core operational hours are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time. Employees are expected to be available for synchronous communication and meetings during this window.
2. **Hybrid Work Model:** Eligible employees may opt for a hybrid work arrangement. The standard expectation is a minimum of two (2) days per week in the primary designated office.
3. **Telecommuting Guidelines:** While working remotely, employees must maintain a secure, private, and ergonomically safe workspace. Use of public Wi-Fi without the Company-approved VPN is strictly prohibited.

---

## 4.0 Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leaves of Absence
The Company believes in work-life integration and provides comprehensive leave benefits to support employee well-being.

### 4.1 Annual Paid Time Off
- **Accrual:** Full-time employees accrue PTO at a rate of 1.66 days per month, totaling 20 days per calendar year.
- **Carryover:** A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the following calendar year, expiring on March 31st of that year.

### 4.2 Sick and Safe Leave
- Employees are provided with up to 10 days of paid sick leave annually. 
- Absences extending beyond three (3) consecutive business days require a formalized medical clearance note from a registered healthcare provider before returning to work.

### 4.3 Parental Leave
- **Primary Caregiver:** Up to sixteen (16) weeks of 100% paid leave for the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- **Secondary Caregiver:** Up to four (4) weeks of 100% paid leave.
- Parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months following the qualifying event.

### 4.4 Bereavement Leave
- Employees are entitled to up to five (5) days of paid leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member.

---

## 5.0 Code of Business Conduct and Ethics
All Employees must adhere to the highest standards of professional conduct.

### 5.1 Conflict of Interest
Employees must avoid situations where personal interests could conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of the Company. Secondary employment or freelance work in a competing industry requires prior written authorization from the HR Department and the Chief Legal Officer.

### 5.2 Anti-Bribery and Corruption
The Company maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards bribery. Employees must not offer, give, solicit, or accept bribes, kickbacks, or any improper payments to secure a business advantage.

### 5.3 Workplace Harassment
Harassment, including sexual harassment, bullying, and intimidation, is strictly prohibited. Any employee who witnesses or experiences harassment must report it immediately to HR or via the anonymous Whistleblower Hotline. All reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially.

---

## 6.0 Information Security and Confidentiality
1. **Confidential Information:** During and after employment, employees must hold all confidential information (including client lists, financial data, source code, and trade secrets) in strict confidence.
2. **Data Protection:** Employees must comply with GDPR, CCPA, and all applicable data privacy laws when handling customer or employee data.
3. **Acceptable Use of Technology:** Company-issued devices are for business use. The Company reserves the right to monitor all activity on corporate networks and devices. Personal software may not be installed on corporate hardware without IT approval.

---

## 7.0 Performance Management and Appraisals
- **Review Cycle:** Formal performance reviews occur bi-annually in June (Mid-Year Check-in) and December (Annual Review).
- **Performance Improvement Plans (PIP):** If an employee's performance falls below expectations, they may be placed on a PIP for a period of 30 to 90 days. Failure to meet the objectives outlined in the PIP may result in termination of employment.

---

## 8.0 Separation of Employment
- **Voluntary Resignation:** Employees are requested to provide a minimum of two (2) weeks' written notice to their direct supervisor and HR.
- **Return of Company Property:** Upon separation, all physical access cards, laptops, and peripheral devices must be returned no later than the final date of employment.
- **Exit Interviews:** Exiting employees will be invited to participate in a confidential exit interview with Human Resources.

---

## 9.0 Policy Violations
Failure to comply with any section of this Human Resources Policy Manual may result in disciplinary action, up to and including immediate termination of employment and, where applicable, civil or criminal prosecution.
