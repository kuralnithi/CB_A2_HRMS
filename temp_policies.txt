
# Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid leave per year. 
Sick leave is unlimited but requires a medical certificate if taking more than 3 consecutive days.
Maternity leave is 6 months paid. Paternity leave is 4 weeks paid.
Public holidays do not count towards the 20 days of paid leave.

# Remote Work Policy
Employees can work remotely up to 3 days a week. 
The remaining 2 days must be spent in the office. 
Core hours are 10 AM to 3 PM, during which employees must be available for meetings.

# Expense Reimbursement
Travel expenses for business trips will be fully reimbursed if approved by the manager beforehand.
Meals during business travel are capped at $50 per day.
Hardware purchases for remote work are covered up to $500 every two years.

# Code of Conduct
Harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.
Employees are expected to treat each other with respect and professionalism.
Conflicts of interest must be reported to HR immediately.

# Performance Review
Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually in June and December.
Raises and promotions are based on performance review outcomes and company financial health.
